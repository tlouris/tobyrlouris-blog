About This Blog

Sharing practical insights from technology leadership in wastewater management

Mission & Purpose

Technology Innovations for the Water Industry is dedicated to sharing practical, real-world insights about digital transformation, cybersecurity, and operational technology in the wastewater sector.

This blog serves as a platform to discuss the challenges and opportunities that come with modernizing critical water infrastructure, implementing new technologies, and navigating an increasingly complex regulatory and threat landscape.

About the Author

Toby R. Louris brings extensive experience in technology leadership within the wastewater industry, with a focus on digital strategy, operational technology security, and enterprise system implementation.

With a background spanning IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, and water utility operations, Toby combines technical expertise with practical industry knowledge to help organizations navigate their digital transformation journeys.

Professional areas of focus include:

  • Digital transformation strategy and roadmap development
  • Operational technology (OT) and SCADA system security
  • IT/OT convergence and integration
  • Cybersecurity compliance and risk management
  • Enterprise technology architecture
  • Technology project management and implementation

What You'll Find Here

Articles on this blog cover four main topics:

Digital Strategy

Guidance on technology planning, innovation adoption, and building organizational capability for sustainable digital transformation.

Cybersecurity & Resilience

Practical approaches to protecting critical infrastructure, securing operational technology, and building resilience against evolving cyber threats.

Project Lessons

Real-world lessons learned from technology implementations, including what worked, what didn't, and what to consider for your own projects.

Industry Trends & Regulation

Analysis of emerging technologies, regulatory developments, compliance requirements, and their implications for water utilities.

Who This Blog is For

This blog is written for:

  • Technology and IT directors at water and wastewater utilities
  • Plant managers and operations leaders exploring technology solutions
  • Consultants working with water utilities on technology projects
  • Vendors and solution providers in the water technology space
  • Anyone interested in the intersection of technology and water infrastructure
